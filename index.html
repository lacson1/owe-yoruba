Òwe Yorùbá

Learn Yoruba proverbs, with translations and meanings

Òwe Yorùbá brings well-known Yoruba proverbs to your iPhone, each with its English translation and meaning. Learn one a day, practise with flashcards and quizzes, and hear each one read aloud.

Support

Questions, problems or a proverb that looks wrong? Email lacsonfree@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. You can also use Suggest a fix on any proverb in the app.

Common questions

Listen doesn't play any sound. Turn up the volume with the side buttons. Listen uses media volume, not the ringer.

Can I get a more natural voice? Yes. On your iPhone, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English and download a Premium voice. Then choose it in the app under Me → Settings → Voice.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your phone. Deleting the app removes it.
