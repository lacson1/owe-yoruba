Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Òwe Yorùbá is run by Lacbis ("we"). This policy covers the website and the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you sign in. We use it only to send sign-in codes and important account messages.
  • Your learning data: progress, streak, badges, favourites, search history and settings. These are saved so they appear on all your devices.
  • Corrections you suggest, which are linked to your account so we can follow up.
  • Payment details, if you subscribe to Pro on the website. Stripe handles these and we never see your card number. We keep only your Stripe customer ID and whether Pro is active.

If you don't sign in, everything stays on your device and nothing is sent to us. We don't use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved data.
  • Vercel hosts the website and runs the server functions.
  • Stripe processes payments.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from the account menu by choosing Delete account. This permanently removes your email, progress and suggestions, and cancels any subscription. To ask for a copy of your data or a correction, email lacsonfree@gmail.com. If you are in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Signing in needs an email address, so children under 13 should sign in with a parent's help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Important changes will be announced in the app.
